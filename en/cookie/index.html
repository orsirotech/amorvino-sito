Cookie policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains which cookies and browser storage the site www.amorvinoenoteca.it uses, under the GDPR and the Italian Data Protection Authority guidelines of 10 June 2021. On your first visit a banner asks what you accept: until you choose, only technical tools are active. Your choice lasts 6 months and you can change it at any time.
