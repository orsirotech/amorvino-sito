The right gift, in six questions

Something to drink or to eat, then the type and the character: round, dry, citrusy, peaty… At the end your request reaches the shop on WhatsApp and we reply with a suggestion.

How a gift is made

  1. 1

    Tell us who it's for

    Come to the shop or call us: who the gift is for, the occasion and how much you want to spend.

  2. 2

    We put it together with you

    We choose the bottles and treats from the shelves, and the type of packaging: hamper, wrapped box or bag.

  3. 3

    Ready to collect

    We wrap it in the shop with your card. For companies we also deliver.

The price depends on what goes inside: we tell you before we make up the gift. Pay in the shop by cash, card or Satispay.
