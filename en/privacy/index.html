Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This notice explains, under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) and the Italian Privacy Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003), which personal data the website www.amorvinoenoteca.it processes, why, on what legal basis, for how long and with which providers.

1. Data controller

Amorvino di Varricchio Domenico (“Amorvino Enoteca e vendita vino”), VAT IT04236010247, Via Vicenza 50/E, Centro Commerciale Le Vele, 36043 Camisano Vicentino (VI), Italy. Email info@amorvino2019.it, phone and WhatsApp +39 0444 212320.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO), as it is not required for our business. For any privacy question, write to the email address above.

2. What data we process, why and on what legal basis

a) Browsing the site

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which delivers it through the Fastly network. These providers automatically log technical visit data (IP address, date and time, requested page, browser type) to run the site and protect it from attacks and abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and available (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

The site has no accounts, sign-up forms, newsletter or online payments, and does not store data on a server of its own.

b) Requests, reservations and quotes via WhatsApp, phone or email

From the site you can message us on WhatsApp (including sending your bottle “list” or the gift questionnaire), call us or email us. We process the data you send — name, phone number, message content, requested products, pickup day, notes, age confirmation — to reply, check availability, prepare your request and, for businesses, issue quotes, invoices and deliveries. Legal basis: pre-contractual or contractual measures requested by you (Art. 6.1.b) and tax and accounting obligations (Art. 6.1.c).

The list and the questionnaire stay on your device until you press “Send” in WhatsApp: the site does not store them on any server. WhatsApp processes messages as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We do not use your data to send you advertising.

c) Statistics (only with your consent)

If you agree in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to learn, in aggregate form, how many people visit the site, which pages they view and which buttons they use (for example “Message us on WhatsApp” or “Directions”). Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6.1.a). You can withdraw it at any time from “Cookie preferences” at the bottom of every page; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before. Details in the cookie policy.

Today the site uses no advertising or profiling tools (such as Google Ads or Meta Pixel). If we enable them, we will update this notice and the banner will ask for your consent before using them.

d) Google map

The map only loads if you press “Show the map”; from then on Google processes browsing data under its own policy. “Directions” links open Google Maps.

e) Reviews shown on the site

We show some reviews that customers published on our Google Business Profile, with a shortened name and the year. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in sharing customers' opinions, as the reviews are already public (Art. 6.1.f). If you wrote a review and do not want it on the site, write to us and we will remove it.

f) Data of corporate gift recipients

When a company asks us to deliver gifts, it gives us the name, address and, if needed, phone number of the people receiving them (data not obtained from the person, Art. 14 GDPR). We use them only to prepare and deliver the gifts. Legal basis: our and the client company's legitimate interest in making the delivery (Art. 6.1.f) and tax obligations for delivery notes and invoices (Art. 6.1.c). The company sharing the data must be entitled to do so.

3. How long we keep data

  • Messages and requests: as long as needed to handle them and, if no purchase follows, no longer than 24 months.
  • Purchase, quote and invoice data: 10 years, as required by tax law.
  • Corporate gift recipients' data: until delivery; if they appear on delivery notes or invoices, 10 years.
  • Google Analytics statistics: 14 months at most; statistics cookies last 2 years at most.
  • Technical browsing data: as long as the hosting providers need it to keep the service secure.

4. Who may receive the data

Only the people who work in the shop and the providers we need, appointed as processors or acting as independent controllers:

  • GitHub, Inc. (site hosting: GitHub Pages) and Fastly, Inc. (network delivering the pages on behalf of GitHub);
  • Aruba S.p.A. (domains and email);
  • WhatsApp Ireland Ltd (WhatsApp messages, when you write to us);
  • Google Ireland Ltd (Google Maps when you use it; Google Analytics only with your consent);
  • our accountant and advisers, for tax and accounting obligations;
  • public authorities, only when required by law.

We never sell or hand over your data.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers (GitHub, Fastly, Google, Meta) may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU‑U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which these providers participate, and/or on the Commission's standard contractual clauses (Articles 45 and 46 GDPR).

6. Is providing data required?

It is optional, but without contact details we cannot answer requests or handle reservations. Refusing statistics cookies does not limit your use of the site in any way.

7. Security

The site only uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The list and the questionnaire stay on your device until you send them. Messages and request data are only accessed by the people who work in the shop.

8. Adults only

The site is about alcoholic drinks and is for adults only. We do not knowingly process data of people under 18; if we find out, we delete it.

9. Our Facebook and Instagram pages

If you follow or message our Facebook and Instagram pages, Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd processes your data under its own policy. For page statistics (Insights), Amorvino and Meta are joint controllers under the agreement provided by Meta; you can exercise your rights on that data directly with Meta.

10. Your rights

You may at any time ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent (Articles 15‑22 GDPR) by writing to info@amorvino2019.it. We reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest. The same rights apply to corporate gift recipients and to review authors.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it). We make no decisions based solely on automated processing and do no profiling.

11. Changes

We may update this notice, for example if we add a service; the date at the top shows the latest version.
