Frequently asked questions

Where is the Amorvino wine shop in Camisano Vicentino?

At Via Vicenza 50/E, Camisano Vicentino (VI), inside the Le Vele shopping centre, between Vicenza and Padua. It's a few minutes' drive from Grisignano di Zocco, Torri di Quartesolo, Piazzola sul Brenta and Veggiano.

What are Amorvino's opening hours?

Tuesday to Saturday 09:00–12:45 and 15:30–19:45; Sunday 09:00–13:00. We are open on Sunday mornings too, when the town market is on; closed on Mondays. Hours may change on public holidays: check the Google listing for updates.

Is there parking near the shop?

Yes, there is free parking in front of the shop at the Le Vele centre, and the entrance is step-free.

Can I order online?

Yes, on WhatsApp: pick the bottles in the catalogue, build your list and send it to the shop (or message us directly). We reply to confirm they are in stock and give you the price. There is no online payment: you pay and collect in the shop by card, Satispay or cash. We deliver corporate gifts ourselves.

What can I buy at Amorvino?

More than 3,000 bottles: red, white and sparkling wines, Veneto first, then Italy and France; natural and biodynamic wines; champagne; craft beers; rum, whisky, gin, tequila, mezcal, grappa, amari and liqueurs; sweet and savoury treats for gifts.

Which Veneto wines do you stock?

Veneto is our largest section: reds, whites and sparkling wines from Veneto wineries, including Lessini Durello and Prosecco. Alongside them are wines from the rest of Italy and from France.

Do you have natural and biodynamic wines from small producers?

Yes, a selection from small growers, with natural and biodynamic wines. In the shop you'll find the ones available at the time.

Which champagne and sparkling wines do you stock?

Champagne from grand maisons and small growers (Dom Pérignon, Perrier-Jouët, Bollinger, Deutz, Bruno Paillard, Jacquesson, Drappier, Charpentier, Gatinois, Diebolt-Vallois), Trentodoc (Giulio Ferrari, Altemasi, Letrari, Cesarini Sforza), Franciacorta (Ca' del Bosco, Uberti, Bellavista), Alta Langa, Sandro De Bruno's Lessini Durello and Col Vetoraz Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore. They are in the site's catalogue.

Which rums do you stock?

A wide and unusual selection: Jamaican pot still rums from Hampden and Appleton (Hearts Collection), Foursquare and Doorly's from Barbados, rhum agricole from Martinique (J. Bally) and Réunion (Savanna), the Haitian clairin Vieux Sajous and vintages from independent bottlers such as Habitation Velier, Papalin and Rum Nation. The bottles are in the site's catalogue.

Do you have rare whisky or peated single malts?

Yes. Scotch single malts such as The Macallan 15 and 18, Highland Park 18 and, among the peated Islay malts, Laphroaig 10, Bowmore 12 and Big Peat; independent bottlings from Signatory Vintage (Mortlach, Caol Ila); Japanese whiskies such as Hibiki, Yamazaki and Nikka; Kentucky bourbon and rye (Michter's, Elijah Craig, Eagle Rare, Heaven Hill) and Indian single malts from Amrut. They are in the site's catalogue.

Do you have craft gin?

Yes, gin is the section with the most labels: nearly ninety bottles. Italian gins such as Alpestre, Poli's Marconi, Engine, Malfy and Gin del Professore; English and Scottish gins such as Hendrick's, Sipsmith, Plymouth, Tarquin's and Silent Pool; Monkey 47 from the Black Forest; Japanese gins such as Roku, Ki No Tea, Ukiyo and Sakurao, and many more from Spain, Greece, Norway and Mexico. They are all in the site's catalogue.

Do you have tequila and mezcal?

Yes: blanco, reposado and extra añejo tequila, almost all 100% agave, from brands such as Los Tres Toños, Lunazul, Ollitas, El Jimador, Batanga and Tapatío, plus mezcal artesanal from Del Maguey and Dangerous Don. They are in the site's catalogue.

Do you have aged grappa?

Yes, including the riserva grappas from the Sibona distillery, finished in Port, Sauternes and rum casks.

Do you make gift boxes and Christmas hampers?

Yes. We make hand-wrapped gift boxes and hampers with wine, spirits and treats, around the budget you give us: we put the box together with you, in the shop.

Do you prepare corporate gifts? Do you deliver?

Yes: gifts for clients and staff, all the same or split by price band, with your own card. We deliver them, or you can collect them in the shop. For a quote, come to the shop or call us.

Can I get an invoice for company purchases?

Yes, we issue invoices on request: just give us your company details when you buy.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards and Satispay, as well as cash.

Can I ask for advice in the shop?

Yes. In the shop you'll find Domenico and Marisa: if you need some advice, just ask. If you're looking for something you can't see on the shelves, ask and we'll tell you straight away.

Can I bring my dog? Is the shop accessible?

Yes to both: dogs are welcome and the entrance is step-free.

Do you speak English in the shop?

Yes. International customers and the American community around Vicenza are welcome: Domenico speaks English.
